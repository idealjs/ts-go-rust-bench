## Reading

- `02-relation-stress` is the **healthiest** category (1.3–7.7×): these are
  the cases where Go itself spends the most time — true algorithmic core,
  and the Rust port is within the same order of magnitude.
- `04-jsx` / `05-module-resolution` are the **largest gaps** (60–208×):
  small Go cost (30ms) vs multi-second Rust runs — the clearest optimization
  targets this suite exposes.
- `01-startup-floor` isolates the fixed pipeline cost: every single-file
  compile in Rust pays a ~1.1s floor vs ~40–210ms in Go.
